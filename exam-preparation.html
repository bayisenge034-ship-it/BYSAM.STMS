Examination Preparation

Practise with self-marking quizzes, then try structured questions and compare your answers with the marking schemes. All questions are original practice questions written for this site, not real examination papers.

Download all practice questions + marking schemes (PDF)

Exam technique in five steps

  1. Read the command word (state, describe, explain, calculate, compare).
  2. Use the marks as a guide: 3 marks means three points or three steps.
  3. Show working – method marks (M) are awarded even when the final answer (A) is wrong.
  4. Check units and significant figures.
  5. Manage time: roughly one minute per mark; leave time to check.

Physics

Quick quiz

Structured question

A 1500 kg car accelerates uniformly from 10 m/s to 30 m/s in 8.0 s.

(a) Calculate the acceleration. [2]   (b) Calculate the resultant force. [2]   (c) Calculate the distance travelled. [2]

Marking scheme (6 marks)
  • (a) a = (v − u)/t [M1] = (30 − 10)/8 = 2.5 m/s² [A1]
  • (b) F = ma [M1] = 1500 × 2.5 = 3750 N [A1] (allow error carried forward from (a))
  • (c) s = ½(u + v)t [M1] = ½ × 40 × 8 = 160 m [A1]
  • Deduct 1 mark once only for a missing or wrong unit.

Mathematics

Quick quiz

Structured question

(a) Solve x² − 7x + 10 = 0. [3]
(b) A straight line passes through (1, 3) and (3, 7). Find its gradient [2] and its equation [2].

Marking scheme (7 marks)
  • (a) Factorise (x − 2)(x − 5) [M1]; x = 2 [A1]; x = 5 [A1]. (Formula method equally acceptable.)
  • (b) Gradient = (7 − 3)/(3 − 1) [M1] = 2 [A1]
  • Equation y − 3 = 2(x − 1) [M1] → y = 2x + 1 [A1]

Chemistry

Quick quiz

Structured question

2.4 g of magnesium reacts with excess hydrochloric acid: Mg + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂ (Ar: Mg 24, Cl 35.5)

(a) Moles of Mg. [1] (b) Moles of H₂. [1] (c) Volume of H₂ at r.t.p. (24 dm³/mol). [1] (d) Mass of MgCl₂ formed. [2]

Marking scheme (5 marks)
  • (a) 2.4 ÷ 24 = 0.1 mol [A1]
  • (b) 1 : 1 ratio, so 0.1 mol [A1]
  • (c) 0.1 × 24 = 2.4 dm³ [A1]
  • (d) Mr(MgCl₂) = 24 + 71 = 95 [M1]; 0.1 × 95 = 9.5 g [A1]

Biology

Quick quiz

Structured question

(a) Define osmosis. [2]
(b) A plant cell is placed in a concentrated sugar solution. Explain what happens to it. [3]
(c) Describe the test for starch. [2]

Marking scheme (7 marks)
  • (a) Movement of water [1] from high to low water potential (dilute to concentrated solution) through a partially permeable membrane [1]
  • (b) Water leaves the cell by osmosis [1]; from the vacuole/cytoplasm [1]; cell becomes flaccid and the membrane pulls away from the cell wall (plasmolysis) [1]
  • (c) Add iodine solution [1]; blue-black colour shows starch present [1]

Using marking schemes

M marks are for correct method, A marks for accurate answers (usually depending on the method mark), and [1] marks for single points. “Error carried forward” means you still get credit if you use your own earlier wrong answer correctly. Mark your own work in a different colour, then redo the questions you lost marks on.
