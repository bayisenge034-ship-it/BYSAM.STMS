Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 18 November 2026

Summary: School records are private to the account that created them. We never sell data and never share student, teacher, marks, attendance or school information with advertisers. Public pages count visits in aggregate without cookies of our own.

1. Who we are

BYSAM.STMS is a free school management system and learning platform (“we”, “us”). You can contact us through the Contact page.

2. School system data

When teachers and authorised users sign in to the school system, they may enter student names, student details, marks, attendance and reports. This data:

  • is stored in the user's own account using Supabase authentication and database access rules (Row Level Security);
  • is visible only to the account that created it and to authorised administrators who manage the platform;
  • is never shared with, sold to, or made visible to advertisers or sponsors;
  • is never used to choose or target advertisements.

No advertisements are displayed on login or registration pages, dashboards, student records, marks, attendance, assessments or reports.

3. Public website analytics

On the public learning pages we count visits, page popularity, resource downloads, and advertisement impressions and clicks. We store only the event type, the page path, the resource or advertisement identifier, and the time. We do not store your IP address, your name, an account identifier or a tracking cookie of our own, and we do not record these events if your browser sends a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal. The figures are used only in aggregate by site administrators.

4. Advertising

Sponsored banners. Administrators may display banners from approved companies and educational organisations on public learning pages. They are labelled “Sponsored”. Clicking one takes you to the sponsor's own website, which has its own privacy policy.

Google AdSense. If and when Google approves this website and an administrator enables it, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies on public learning pages to serve and measure advertisements, including personalised ads where permitted. You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage ad personalisation in your Google account's ad settings. Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before personalised advertising is shown.

5. Children

Learning pages are suitable for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children on the public pages. Student records in the school system are entered by teachers and schools, who are responsible for having the right to do so.

6. Security

We use authenticated access, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and database access rules to protect data. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password and sign out on shared computers.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data, including under applicable data protection law such as Rwanda's Law No. 058/2021 on the protection of personal data and privacy. To make a request, contact us.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the “last reviewed” date when we do.

This policy is provided in good faith as a plain-language statement of our practices. It is not legal advice; the platform owner should have it reviewed by a qualified adviser for their jurisdiction.
