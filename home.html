100% free for schools

A free school management system, and a free place to learn.

BYSAM.STMS helps teachers record marks and attendance, produce report cards and certificates, and understand how their classes are performing. Alongside it, the public Learning Resources section offers study notes, worked examples, practice exams and lesson plans for students and teachers.

Why schools use BYSAM.STMS

Free, with no catch for schools

There are no subscription fees, no payment walls and no paid accounts. The platform is supported by advertising on its public learning pages only.

Private school data

Each teacher's records are saved in their own account. Student information, marks and attendance are never shown to advertisers and no ads appear in the school system.

Built for busy teachers

Enter marks once and get totals, grades, rankings and class analysis automatically. Works on a phone, tablet or computer.

Learning for everyone

Free notes and practice for Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry and Biology, with worked examples and marking schemes.

What the school system can do

  • Classes and students – set up classes, import student lists from Excel or PDF, and keep student information together.
  • Marks and assessments – continuous assessment and examination marks with configurable weights and grade boundaries.
  • Attendance – mark present, late or absent and see attendance rates.
  • Report cards and certificates – generate printable PDF reports and certificates of achievement.
  • Analysis – charts and statistics to spot strengths and students who need support.
  • Backup – download an extra copy of your data at any time.

How BYSAM.STMS stays free

Running a platform costs money, but schools should not have to pay for it. BYSAM.STMS may show clearly labelled advertisements and sponsored educational content on its public learning pages. Advertisers are reviewed by an administrator, and they never receive any student, teacher, marks, attendance or school information. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
