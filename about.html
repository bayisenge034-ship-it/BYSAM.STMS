About BYSAM.STMS

BYSAM.STMS is a free School Management System and educational platform. It exists so that any school, however small or under-resourced, can keep good records and so that any learner can find good study material.

Our mission

To give schools, teachers and students free, reliable and easy-to-use digital tools for managing school records and for learning, without charging schools or compromising their data.

Our vision

A future where every school can run its academic administration digitally, where teachers spend their time teaching rather than calculating averages, and where quality learning resources are open to all students.

Our objectives

  • Keep the school system free for all schools, teachers and authorised users.
  • Make marks, attendance, reports and certificates fast and accurate.
  • Protect school data: records stay private and are never shared with advertisers.
  • Publish free learning resources in Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry and Biology, with exam practice and teaching support.
  • Work well on low-cost phones and slow connections.

Purpose and how it is funded

The school system and the learning resources are free to use. To cover running costs, the public learning pages may carry advertising: Google AdSense once the site is approved, and sponsored banners from companies and educational organisations that an administrator has approved. Advertising never appears in the school system (login, registration, dashboards, student records, marks, attendance, assessments or reports), and advertisers never see school data.

About our learning content

Learning resources are written as study support. They are designed to complement, not replace, your teacher, textbook and official syllabus. If you spot an error, please tell us.
